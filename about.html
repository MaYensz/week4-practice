About Ironwood Woodworking Co.

Ironwood Woodworking Co. started in a one-car garage with a single table saw and a lot of firewood-grade mistakes. Ten years later the shop has grown into a small storefront with three full-time woodworkers, but the actual approach hasn't changed: one piece at a time, built for the person who ordered it, not for a warehouse shelf.

Every table, chair, and cabinet that leaves the shop is solid wood, joined the way furniture was joined before particleboard and staples took over the industry: mortise and tenon joints, dovetailed drawers, real wood glue. It takes longer than a factory line. It also means a piece we build today should still be standing, and still be worth repairing instead of replacing, in fifty years.
