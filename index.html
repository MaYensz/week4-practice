Handmade Furniture Built to Last Generations

Ironwood Woodworking Co. is a small furniture shop building solid wood tables, chairs, and cabinetry one piece at a time. Every project starts with a real conversation about how you actually plan to use the piece, not a catalog number you pick off a shelf.

We work mostly in white oak, walnut, and maple, sourced from sustainably managed forests within driving distance of the shop. Nothing here is built from particleboard or veneer. If you can put a hand on it, it's solid wood underneath.

Stop by the shop Tuesday through Saturday, 9am to 5pm, or send us a message through our contact page to talk through a custom piece. You can also follow our latest projects on Instagram.
